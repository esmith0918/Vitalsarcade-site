Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 20, 2026

Vitals Arcade ("we," "us") makes educational games and VR experiences, including Acid-Base Arcade. This policy explains what information our games and this website collect and how we use it.

Information our games collect

Our games do not ask for your name, email address, or other personal information. Game progress and high scores are saved on your headset. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Information collected by platforms

When you download or play our games through the Meta Horizon Store, itch.io, or SideQuest, those platforms may collect information under their own privacy policies, such as your account details, purchase history, and device information. We may receive basic, non-identifying sales and usage reports from those platforms to help us improve our games.

This website

This website does not use advertising trackers, cookies for tracking, or accounts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser type, for security and performance. The playable demo on our homepage runs entirely in your browser and does not send your score anywhere. When you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

Children

Our games are designed for teens and adults ages 13 and up, especially nursing and health students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Educational use

Our games are study aids. They are not medical advice and are not a substitute for your coursework, instructors, or clinical training.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
