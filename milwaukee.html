Why this matters

We are moving from the Information Age, the age of computers and the internet, into the Intelligence Age, where AI helps people think, learn and solve problems. Every time the world changed like this, the people who learned the new tools moved ahead. The people left out fell behind.

What you will learn

Every class is hands-on, with phones in hand and one real task each time. The lessons follow the five big questions of AI literacy from research by Long and Magerko (2020).

  • What is AI?What it is in plain words, and what it is not.
  • What can AI do?Write a letter or resume, plan a budget, help with homework, start a side business.
  • How does AI work?It learns patterns from lots of examples. That is why it can sound sure and still be wrong.
  • How should AI be used?Staying safe from scam calls and fake voices, protecting your privacy, checking answers.
  • How do people feel about AI?Talking openly about fears, hopes and fairness, including what is happening here in Wisconsin.

If we sit on the sidelines

  • Scams hit our families hardest
  • New jobs go to people with the skills
  • Kids learn AI with no guidance at home
  • Choices about AI get made without us

If we learn together

  • Families know the tricks and stay safe
  • Neighbors qualify for new and better jobs
  • Parents guide kids to use AI the right way
  • Our community helps shape how AI is used
